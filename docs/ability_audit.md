## Interaction reports (per spec)

- **Phase B padded route trainer mons**: `B_TRAINER_MON_RANDOM_ABILITY` is
  FALSE in `include/config/battle.h`, and padded mons have no explicit
  `Ability:` line, so they are locked to slot 1 (primary). Per the spec this
  is left as-is; future variant teams can opt into the new abilities with
  explicit lines.
- **Wild mons**: `CreateBoxMon` rolls only slots 0/1 from the personality
  (gen 3-4 formula) and only when slot 2 is non-empty — so wild mons pick up
  new secondary abilities naturally, and hidden abilities remain exclusive to
  the Ability Coach.
