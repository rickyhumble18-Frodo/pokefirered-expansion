## Rev3 fix summary

1. Cosmetic families collapsed to one decision per stage (Alcremie/Furfrou/Flabebe-Floette-Florges/Scatterbug-Spewpa-Vivillon/Deerling-Sawsbuck/Squawkabilly/Tatsugiri) — the rest inherit.
2. Universal cap lowered to 25/ability; Tinted Lens & Analytic now bound by it; decision justifications capped at 12 identical strings (mechanical inherit/cosmetic/exempt strings excepted, being intentionally identical).
3. Huge/Pure Power gated on the species' OWN Atk>=SpA + a STAB physical move it actually learns; removed from Ditto (Transform overwrites it); spec-named special lines hard-excluded.
